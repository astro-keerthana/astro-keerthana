## 🧬 What I Work On

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### Disease Omics
RNA expression profiling and in-silico study design, looking for molecular signatures that cross disease boundaries — periodontitis and Alzheimer's, oral dysbiosis and cardiovascular risk.

`transcriptomics` · `disease-gene association` · `pathway interpretation`

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### Space Life Sciences
How microbes, fungi and human physiology behave in space-relevant environments — mutation risk, biosignatures, lunar regolith, and engineered countermeasures.

`astromicrobiology` · `comparative genomics` · `biosignatures`

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### Bio-Intelligence Systems
Physiology meets hardware: interfacing wearable sensing with real biological signals, selecting the attributes that actually carry information, and validating the science behind the product.

`physiological data` · `sensor validation` · `signal attributes`

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### Open & Citizen Science
Time-domain astrophysics, exoplanet observation and life-sciences datasets — contributed to and reviewed in public, with communities rather than behind a paywall.

`GOTO / Kilonova Seekers` · `NASA OSDR` · `EXOTIC`

</td>
</tr>
</table>
